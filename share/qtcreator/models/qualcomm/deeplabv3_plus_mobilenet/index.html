DeepLabV3-Plus-MobileNet-Quantized

Quantized Deep Convolutional Neural Network model for semantic segmentation. DeepLabV3 Quantized is designed for semantic segmentation at multiple scales, trained on various datasets. It uses MobileNet as a backbone.
